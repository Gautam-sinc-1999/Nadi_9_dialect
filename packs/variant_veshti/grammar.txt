Veshti Grammar Sketch
Compiled by R. Osei, 2021.

Word order. The verb comes first in the sentence, before the subject and the object. Time words usually come at the end.

Tense. The past tense is marked with the prefix ka- on the verb. The present tense has no prefix. A future tense prefix exists but examples are few.

Negation. The negation particle mo follows the verb directly.

Questions. A yes/no question begins with the particle ne. The verb still comes right after ne.

Politeness. Use torin for elders and guests, and tor for friends and children. There is no verb agreement.

Plural. Plural nouns are not documented.
